## Which formats get traction (before/after, sped-up screen recording, 30-sec tips, myth busting, tool reveals, comment-to-DM)?

### Takeaway
Only generic international evidence found; no Hebrew-specific performance data.

### Cited Findings
- Comment-keyword CTA (e.g. "comment HOOK") with automated DM delivering a resource is a common recommended pattern, but the source is a DM-automation vendor (self-interested) — [Inrō](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-3-second-hook-leads)
- Ranking signals cited: watch time, likes, sends (shares via DM) — [CapCut IG algorithm (Hebrew)](https://www.capcut.com/he-il/resource/instagram-algorithm); [Inrō](https://www.inro.social/blog/how-instagram-reels-work-2025)
- CapCut Hebrew resource hub publishes short-form tool articles — [CapCut Hebrew editing tips](https://www.capcut.com/he-il/resource/editing-tips?pageNo=3)

### Inferences
- Comment-to-get-link plus "send to a friend" are consistent with the sends/DM signal, but no Israeli conversion data exists in the sources.

### Gaps
- No evidence on before/after, myth-busting, sped-up screen recording performance in Hebrew. No Meta policy check on DM automation was found; verify before relying on it.

## Hooks, caption styles, reel length and CTA patterns in Hebrew

### Takeaway
International sources: open with the payoff, no intro; vendor claims on length/retention are weakly sourced. No Hebrew hook corpus found.

### Cited Findings
- Skip intros, lead with payoff/tension/contrast/promised result; vendor-cited ~50% drop-off in first 3 seconds and a 60% 3-second-hold target (unverified) — [Inrō](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-3-second-hook-leads); [BlitzCut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-instagram-reels-hooks-2026)
- Reels can run up to 3 minutes; one blog claims 7-90s has best viral potential (weak source) — [Metricool guide](https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-guide/)
- Hebrew guidance: cut the start until the action/message appears, B-roll only to illustrate, shoot 9:16, keep titles/logo away from app UI — [Emojo](https://emojo.co.il/%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%93%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%AA-%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%A1-%D7%91%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%98%D7%92%D7%A8%D7%9D/)
- Hebrew auto-captions: CapCut, VEED, Kapwing, HeyGen offer them (vendor pages; no independent Hebrew accuracy test found) — [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/he-il/tools/auto-caption-app); [VEED](https://www.veed.io/he-IL/tools/auto-subtitle-generator-online); [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/he/subtitles/editor)

### Inferences
- Hebrew auto-caption accuracy and RTL rendering are likely real pain points a tool could address (no source confirms this).

### Gaps
- Actual Hebrew hook wording, caption styles, typical reel length of top Israeli editing accounts: not found. No independent optimal-length data found.
